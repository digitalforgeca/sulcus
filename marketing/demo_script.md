*   **Action:** User inputs: "Julian is the architect for Aethelgard."
*   **Narrator:** "Every AI agent remembers the last thing you said. But what happens after an hour of work?"

*   **Prompt:** "Who is Julian and what project is he on?"

*   **Action:** Open a terminal window running `sulcus list-hot`. 
*   **Visual:** Show the "Aethelgard" and "Julian" nodes with 0.0 heat.
*   **Prompt:** "Who is leading my metaverse project?"
*   **SULCUS INTERNALS (Overlay):** Show a flash of heat (1.0) hitting the Aethelgard node. Show the heat "bleeding" through the graph to the Julian node.
*   **Narrator:** "Sulcus detects the semantic relationship. It ignites the cold memory, diffusing heat through the knowledge graph to find related facts."
